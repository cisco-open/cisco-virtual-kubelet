    Rules for execution and closure

    • Use native Kubernetes scheduling, controllers, Eviction/PDB, admission and RBAC, plus CVK's own manager/workers. No third-party scheduler or workflow controller. Keep the two functional worker accounts with RO/RW profiles; separately bind planning, activation approval and recovery authority.
    • Do not rebuild completed observation acceptance, grant fencing, staging, approval or E10 graph features. Add the missing tests first; change the owning layer only when a failing test or missing contract warrants it.
    • Continue with Ubuntu16 and physical 198.51.100.100, .101, .103 only after rechecking serial numbers, device/Node UIDs and management ownership. Exclude CI devices, the historical duplicate cat9k-node, and unqualified Nexus/IOS-XR targets from positive C9K results. Do not infer a physical path or spare app capacity from three Ready Nodes.
    • Native scheduling selects application placement. CVK authorizes network disruption from administrator policy, accepted observations and atomic budgets; TAS binding or a complete graph alone cannot authorize an upgrade.
    • Every detailed E/F gate must end as PASS, FAIL, BLOCKED or a justified NOT_APPLICABLE, with candidate, cohort and evidence. Missing hardware is BLOCKED, not PASS. Conditional durable-cache tests may be N/A after the measured decision; missing second-platform or physical group support may not be silently scoped away to claim the complete roadmap.
