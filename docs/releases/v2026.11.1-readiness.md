## Why the previous candidate was rejected

`v2026.11.0` was tagged and staged, but exact inspection of its linux/arm64
controller image found the MPL-labelled `scalar-parcels.json` and
`classic-templates.json` embedded in the executable without corresponding
copied source or attribution in `/licenses`. The inspected image digest was
`sha256:3426c740728aaa4b34ace434b9f4e62f1a5ccca2c838032a2146a3b8a83c94d2`.

That failed the existing **Licensing is a hard gate** requirement. The original
[notes](v2026.11.0.md) and [preparation record](v2026.11.0-readiness.md) remain
historical. Never publish that draft, move/delete the tag, or replace its staged
artifacts. October remains the published baseline. This was an artifact
packaging finding; it does not change the recorded platform qualification.
